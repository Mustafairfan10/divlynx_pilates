## Scope

Public site of Divlynx Pilates: landing (`/`), shop (`/shop`), first-visit intake (`/join`), plus the reserve dialog, bag and the client/staff view switch. Mode: Persuade (landing, shop) with an Operate-grade form (`/join`).

## Audience, job, action

Curious first-timers who have never done Pilates and feel intimidated. They must understand exactly what a first class is, what it costs and what to bring, then act: write their name and start the intake form, take the intro offer, or reserve a real class. Proof is the studio working: live timetable spots, real prices and policies from app data. No testimonials or ratings exist; never invent them.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the personal programme sheet an instructor hands a beginner at their first session — printed form, pencil exercise figures, ballpoint notes in the margin. It refuses the wellness brochure: photo hero, headline over button, three cards, testimonials, cream and serif.

OWN-WORLD: Bright white bond paper with pre-printed form furniture in form-blue (ruled lines, boxes, condensed caps labels in Archivo narrow); printed text near-black in Archivo (expanded heavy for titles, condensed caps for labels); running body copy in Atkinson Hyperlegible Next — an adaptation cited to PRODUCT.md Accessibility & Inclusion (injured, pregnant and low-vision readers), set as the form's typed text; graphite-grey stick figures; ballpoint-blue handwriting (Reenie Beanie) for ticks, circles, arrows, instructor notes; fluorescent highlighter yellow marks every primary action; carbonless copy colours (canary, pink) own whole sections; a magenta clinic stamp for the intro offer; perforated tear-off stub as footer.

STORY: Understand what happens in a first class and that it is built for beginners; believe a real instructor will tailor it (notes, intake reaches them); do: write your name, continue to the intake, pick up the intro offer, reserve a live slot.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The whole viewport is the top of the sheet. Printed header strip (studio, "Client programme", form no., today's date in ballpoint). Left ~60%: "Your first Pilates programme" at display scale, then "Prescribed for" with a live name field that writes in ballpoint as you type, beside a highlighted "Start my programme" button (→ /join prefilled). Below it the first entries of the numbered programme with pencil figures and the next real slot shown as capacity boxes. Right margin ~30%: instructor's ballpoint notes and arrows pointing at entry 1, plus the stamped intro-offer price.

FORM: Prescription sheet (physio exercise handout), #1 of my ordered list; chosen by the user as IMPECCABLE'S PICK. seed:010546341fb7b4d0

SIGNATURE: Ink being written — the name field fills in handwriting, checkboxes receive drawn ballpoint ticks, booking crosses a capacity box, highlighter swipes draw once in reading order.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

Read studio name, prices, classes, instructors, hours, policy from app data. Handwriting is never the only carrier of essential information. Respect reduced motion. Client ↔ staff view switch always one tap away on both sides. Demo content labelled as such.
